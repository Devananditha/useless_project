## 4. Definitive Committee Evaluation Against Five Core Questions

The judging committee evaluated the concepts against five explicit comparative questions:

### 4.1 Which concept is stronger overall?
**Answer: CrossBorder TitleGuard.**  
While PartsRescue AI is an exceptional concept that scores 4.1150, TitleGuard achieves **4.6450** due to its flawless operational white space, complete data independence from gated vendor APIs, and superior presentation immediacy.

### 4.2 Which concept is easier and more reliable to demonstrate?
**Answer: CrossBorder TitleGuard.**  
TitleGuard's demo consists of feeding high-resolution scanned titles through a fast multimodal VLM and evaluating local AST statutory rules in under 50 milliseconds. It can be demonstrated with 100% deterministic reliability on stage. In contrast, PartsRescue AI requires orchestrating multiple simulated REST endpoints (parts catalogs, warehouse inventory, technician queues, and customer SMS dialogues), increasing live demonstration latency and failure risk.

### 4.3 Which concept has greater real-world dealership impact?
**Answer: Nuanced — TitleGuard for Capital Liquidity; PartsRescue for Operating Profit.**  
* If judging by **operating gross profit**, PartsRescue AI generates **$128,400/yr in net gross profit** versus TitleGuard's **$59,605/yr in net cash savings**.
* If judging by **enterprise capital liquidity and contract integrity**, TitleGuard liberates **$420,000 in frozen monthly working capital**, prevents customer trade unwinds, and eliminates dealer licensing penalties.
* TitleGuard's financial return is also **immune to demand-absorption risk**—every title defect prevented directly accelerates cash, whereas recovered bay hours require customer work orders to monetize.

### 4.4 Which concept has more defensible differentiation against competitors?
**Answer: CrossBorder TitleGuard.**  
Every incumbent titling solution (Dealertrack RegUSA, Solera PA Titling) operates as a **post-sale back-office bureau**. TitleGuard is the only solution operating as a **pre-contract intake gate at showroom desking**. Furthermore, within Tekion's ecosystem, TitleGuard fills the exact paper loophole between Deal Guard AI and TDP without overlapping either. In contrast, PartsRescue AI must constantly defend against appearing as an external wrapper around Tekion's native ARC Parts Cloud and Service Cloud.

### 4.5 Which concept would we personally choose as hackathon participants?
**Answer: CrossBorder TitleGuard.**  
As hackathon competitors who must pitch a winning project in 5 minutes with maximum credibility and zero presentation failures:
1. We can build and verify TitleGuard using **100% authentic, publicly verifiable state statutory codes** without apologizing for mocked supplier databases.
2. We can show a crumpled, scanned Arizona title on screen and visually flag an unnotarized bill of sale in 8 seconds, creating an immediate, undeniable visual victory.
3. We avoid the dangerous trap of pitching a service parts tool to Tekion engineers who already work daily on Tekion ARC Parts Cloud.
