## A. Executive Verdict

Following an exhaustive, evidence-driven adversarial audit across patent claims, competitive boundaries, multi-tier agentic feasibility, and unified financial models:

1. **Both concepts are genuine, high-value contenders:**
   * **Concept A (TitleGuard)** is an exceptionally well-scoped **dealership trade-in document intake pre-audit assistant** that solves a direct, binary $336k capital float freeze at the showroom sales desk, using deterministic tri-state corridor statutes (CA-NV-AZ).
   * **Concept B (PartsRescue AI)** is an elegant **service parts shortage recovery agent** that solves in-bay repair stalls by orchestrating multi-echelon sourcing, supersession traversal, and technician labor resequencing, directly matching official **Theme 1 UC04**.
2. **Definitive Committee Determination:**  
   **CrossBorder TitleGuard (Concept A) is recommended as the primary candidate concept (Score: 4.2900 vs 3.9850).** While PartsRescue generates higher nominal operating gross profit ($92.8k vs $48.1k), TitleGuard is decisively superior in **hackathon data autonomy** (authentic public statutes vs paywalled OEM parts APIs), **5-minute live demonstration reliability**, **Tekion ecosystem white space**, and **compound downside survivability**.
3. **The User Retains Final Authority:** Under project rules, this recommendation is advisory. The USER retains exclusive authority to approve the final concept freeze before Phase 5 begins.
