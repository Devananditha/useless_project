6. **Demo Potential (10%):** Visual impact, immediacy, and comprehension in a 5-minute hackathon pitch.
7. **Adoption Practicality (5%):** Alignment with dealership frontline incentives and absence of employee workflow resistance.
8. **Evidence Strength (10%):** Grounding in traceable primary statutes, NADA benchmarks, and verified trade studies.

## 3. Identification of Subjective Ratings & Scoring Rationale

To ensure complete transparency under `.cursor/rules/research-standards.mdc`, the following subjective score assignments were audited:

1. **TitleGuard Novelty (4.70):** Supported by Audit C competitive intelligence, which confirmed that existing titling incumbents (Dealertrack RegUSA, Solera TitleTec, Vitu) operate *post-sale back-office clearinghouses*. Automated, real-time multimodal VLM pre-auditing of physical paper titles during showroom intake is a confirmed enterprise white space. (Subjectivity risk: LOW).
2. **TitleGuard Demo Potential (5.00):** Rated 5.00 because visual document grounding (scanned PDF with red bounding box overlays) is objectively the most commanding format for a 5-minute presentation. (Subjectivity risk: LOW).
3. **BayFlow Novelty (3.20 vs Initial 4.80):** In Phase 3 initial scoring, BayFlow was awarded 4.80 for novelty based on Google OR-Tools CP-SAT math. The Innovation Critic red-team review rightfully demoted this to 3.20 because flexible job-shop scheduling is standard operations research literature, and commercial DMS dispatch boards (CDK ShopTrak, Reynolds, Dealer-FX) already implement dispatch algorithms. (Demotion verified as sound).
4. **BayFlow Tekion Fit (3.40 vs Initial 4.70):** Tekion ARC Service Cloud publicly advertises "Smart Tech Matching" and live bay capacity tracking. Proposing a separate dispatch solver risks direct friction with Tekion engineering judges. (Demotion verified as sound).
5. **BayFlow Adoption (3.20 vs Initial 3.60):** Automotive service technicians operate on flat-rate compensation. Algorithms that unilaterally reassign flag-hour jobs create fierce union and technician pushback. (Demotion verified as sound).
