## 1. Methodology: Multi-Variable Combined Stress Testing

Single-variable sensitivity tests can produce misleading optimism by assuming all other variables remain stable. In reality, operational and technical failures correlate:
* A concept with patent vulnerability often faces simultaneous evidentiary skepticism.
* A concept with proprietary data barriers often suffers increased implementation complexity.

This audit executes **multi-variable compound stress tests** on both concepts to establish which survives severe, adverse judging conditions.

## 3. Analysis of Compound Downside Scenarios

### 3.1 Scenario 5: Simultaneous Combined Downside (Both Suffer Adverse Conditions)
* **TitleGuard Under Simultaneous Downside:**
  * Novelty reduced by -0.8 (due to aggressive patent pushback by IP judges).
  * Data Availability reduced by -0.6 (penalized for inability to call live DMV APIs).
  * Business Value reduced by -0.5 (penalized for non-preventable deal delays).
  * **Resulting Downside Score:** **3.9650 / 5.00**
* **PartsRescue Under Simultaneous Downside:**
  * Data Availability reduced by -0.8 (penalized for relying entirely on mock supplier endpoints).
  * Business Value reduced by -0.8 (penalized for 50% demand absorption in slow service bays).
  * Technical Feasibility reduced by -0.6 (penalized for complex multi-echelon integration barriers).
  * **Resulting Downside Score:** **3.6550 / 5.00**
* **Downside Finding:** When both concepts are hit with simultaneous negative assumptions, **TitleGuard retains Rank 1 by +0.3100 points**. TitleGuard has fewer moving parts and relies on public statutes, giving it higher baseline survivability.

## 6. Audit Conclusion

The combined sensitivity analysis demonstrates that:
1. **TitleGuard is the safer, more resilient base-case concept (4.2900 vs 3.9850)**, surviving simultaneous compound downside scenarios due to its data autonomy and zero demand-absorption volatility.
2. **PartsRescue AI is the higher-ceiling, more modern agentic concept**, possessing higher commercial operating gross profit ($92.8k vs $48.1k) and superior novelty (4.10 vs 3.90).
3. If hackathon judges focus strictly on **agentic depth and Theme 1 UC04 alignment**, PartsRescue can decisively overtake TitleGuard.
