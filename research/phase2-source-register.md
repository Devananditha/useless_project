## 2. Evidence Categorization & Traceability Standards

All entries in this register satisfy the following criteria:
1. **Verifiable Authorship:** Sourced from primary industry trade bodies (NADA, FTC), dominant automotive market research institutions (Cox Automotive, McKinsey), specialized telemetry/inventory providers (vAuto, Recurrent, ATC, AutoAlert), or documented operational publications (CBT News, Automotive News).
2. **Methodological Separation:** Quantitative operational benchmarks (e.g., $466 RO average, 10–12.2 days frontline latency, 15%–25% appointment no-shows) are distinguished from marketing claims or vendor software feature summaries.
3. **Traceability:** Direct primary domains and publications are cited for cross-examination during red-team and validation phases.
